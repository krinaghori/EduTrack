Common Learning Gaps & Academic Difficulties

Critical Deficit: Math Multiplication Tables & Division

Students in the bottom quartile rely on finger-counting for tables 6 through 9 and fail to grasp division as repeated subtraction.

Recommended: Daily 10-minute dot array matrix drills + abacus kit practice.

Language Gap: Matra Confusion (હ્રસ્વ 'િ' vs દીર્ઘ 'ી')

Gujarati and Hindi writing shows persistent vowel matra swaps altering word meanings (e.g. દિન vs દીન).

Recommended: Barakhadi audio rhymes and tactile sandpaper letter tracing.

Commendable Mastery: EVS Plant & Living World

High student engagement in plant photosynthesis and community helper roles (Class mastery exceeds 82%).

Recommended: Leverage nature projects to cross-teach vocabulary and measurements.
